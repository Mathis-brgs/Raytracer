# Code of conduct

We want a project where everyone can take part without harassment, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Do

- Be kind and patient. Many of us are learning.
- Respect other views and give feedback that helps.
- Accept feedback on your own work, and own your mistakes.

## Do not

- Use sexual language or make unwanted advances.
- Troll, insult or attack people.
- Harass anyone, in public or in private.
- Share someone's private information without their permission.

## Where it applies

In all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may ban people who break it repeatedly or seriously.

To report a problem, write to mathis.bc23@gmail.com. Reports are handled privately.
